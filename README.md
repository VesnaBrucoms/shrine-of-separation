# Shrine of Separation

Small mod with a simple dungeon and short quest, for my beloved. <3

I've made this publically available as I have some scripting that might be useful as reference. However,
this can't be downloaded anywhere except from here as it's very rough and exceptionally cheesy. I doubt anyone
would fancy playing this let alone enjoy it. After all, I made this for my partner to be played on Valentines
Day!

You are free to make use of the one model that I made in your mods, providing that you credit me
appropriately.

Script actions that may be of interest:
* Moving PC upon equipping a ring
* OnDeath of a creature, lower a gate
* Placing a soul gem into an inventory to trigger a "resurrection"
